# look-and-feel.md — StressFreeClaim.ai

Thin by design — the per-project design-tokens skill generates the real identity from these adjectives.

## Name & framing (settled)

Front the app as **StressFreeClaim.ai**. **Deskar 6 and Nexus stay behind the scenes** — the founder: *"Part of the marketing focus is in the name."* The name is the promise: this is the stress-free way to file.

## Adjectives

Calm · reassuring · effortless · plain-spoken. The person opening this just went through a hurricane, **the power is out**, and their phone battery is low. It should feel like someone competent taking the whole thing off their plate — a steady hand, not a form.

## Anti-direction

Not a hard-sell contractor/roofer aesthetic, not insurance-corporate cold, not the generic AI-default look (purple gradients, glassmorphism). The founder's world is door-knocking sales — the app should feel like the opposite of being sold to. Nothing that reads as a long form, a wizard with a progress bar, or a paperwork chore.

## References

None supplied. The founder gives framing ("convenience," "concierge service," the name itself) but no brand or visual references. *(Distiller did not invent brand references; generate the identity from the adjectives.)*

## Voice (copy)

Warm, concierge, minimal. The opening line does the heavy lifting: *"Tell me what happened and we'll take it from there."* Ask for as little as possible — every extra question is a reason for someone without power to give up. When something's unclear, ask plainly and briefly. No sales pressure and **no fee talk** (the fee/waiver messaging is deliberately out of the prototype). Empty and error states give direction, not mood — and a failed parse must never dead-end the user.
